About PadyakHub

Started by passionate cyclists for passionate cyclists. Serving the Philippine biking community since 2015.

Our Story

PadyakHub was founded in 2015 in Valenzuela City — the biking capital of the Philippines — by a group of passionate cyclists who saw a need for a reliable, affordable, and expert-driven bike shop.

From a small garage operation, we've grown into one of the most trusted names in the Philippine cycling community, offering thousands of parts, professional repair services, and same-day delivery across Metro Manila.

Our mechanics have a combined 50+ years of experience and are certified by major brands including Shimano, SRAM, and Campagnolo.
